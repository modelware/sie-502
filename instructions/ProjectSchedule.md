# Project Submission Schedule

Assignments are submitted weekly. Projects are numbered separately because several project
submissions combine work from two weeks.

| Project | Weeks included | Work completed | Submission point |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Project 1](Week1.md#project-1-week-1-project-scope-problem-statement) | Week 1 | Scope and problem statement | End of Week 1 |
| [Project 2](Week2.md#project-2-weeks-2-3-week-2-component-initial-vocabulary-and-description) | Weeks 2-3 | Initial vocabulary and description, followed by reasoning features | End of Week 3, as one cumulative submission |
| [Project 3](Week4.md#project-3-weeks-4-5-week-4-component-turn-your-vocabulary-into-a-small-methodology) | Weeks 4-5 | Methodology and editors, followed by the analysis layer | End of Week 5, as one cumulative submission |
| [Project 4](Week6.md#project-4-week-6-put-an-agent-to-work-on-your-method) | Week 6 | Agent experiment on the student's methodology | End of Week 6 |
| [Project 5](Week7.md#project-5-weeks-7-8-week-7-component-generate-publish-and-reuse-your-methodology) | Weeks 7-8 | Generate, publish, reuse, and complete the methodology work | End of Week 8, as one cumulative submission |

For a project spanning two weeks, the first week begins the work and the second week completes
it. Submit the latest cumulative commit containing both weeks. Do not submit or grade each week
as a separate project.
